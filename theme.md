# Flowbase Cozy UI Theme

## Personality
Flowbase should feel fresh, calm, and useful: a crisp workspace that blends structured notes, visual thinking, and AI support. Keep the coziness in the softness of spacing and surfaces, not in dull or dusty colors.

## Color Palette
- App background: clear near-white aqua, `hsl(188 80% 98%)`, with fresh teal and sky washes on large dashboard surfaces.
- Surfaces: clean white cards and sidebar, `hsl(0 0% 100%)`.
- Text: deep ink, `hsl(224 34% 12%)`.
- Muted text: cool readable gray, `hsl(224 15% 36%)`.
- Borders: crisp low-contrast blue gray, `hsl(198 48% 86%)`; prefer subtle opacity on dense UI.
- Primary: vivid teal, `hsl(174 84% 32%)`.
- Primary soft: bright pale teal, `hsl(174 88% 92%)`.
- Accent wash: fresh mint, `hsl(171 86% 94%)`.
- Icon accents: coral/rose, teal, sky, violet, amber, emerald, fuchsia, and indigo.

## Typography
- Use the system sans stack by default for fast rendering and a native app feel.
- Dashboard page titles should be calm and medium-sized, around 24px.
- Sidebar labels should be compact, around 12px, with group labels around 8.5px uppercase.
- Keep letter spacing at `0` for normal text; use slight positive spacing only for tiny uppercase group labels.

## Spacing And Shape
- Use 8px radius for cards, buttons, panels, and sidebar rows.
- Prefer compact spacing in repeated navigation and dense productivity surfaces.
- Use 20px to 24px padding for main dashboard panels.
- Avoid nested cards; use repeated cards for comparable content only.
- Use soft, low-opacity shadows only to separate surfaces from the fresh background.

## Sidebar Guidelines
- Expanded width: about 196px.
- Collapsed width: about 60px.
- Collapsed state shows icons only and hides text, app subtitle, group labels, and footer copy.
- Menu rows should be 28px tall with compact icon tiles.
- Icons should stay colorful to help users recognize destinations quickly, but keep icon backgrounds pale.
- Group labels should be short and scannable, such as Plan, Create & Map, and Manage.
- The footer should act like a quiet workspace status module when expanded and a single centered icon when collapsed.

## Design Rules
- The first screen should be the working product experience, not a landing page.
- Keep the palette balanced; avoid making the app feel mostly beige, dark slate, or purple.
- Use fresh color as accents, not as large decorative backgrounds.
- UI copy should be direct and functional.
- Keep the mood cozy without dulling the interface: use clear contrast, fresh teal, and crisp white surfaces.
